Expert Landscape Design, Garden Installations, Outdoor Construction, And More!

At J&P LANDSCAPING AND LOWN LLC, we help homeowners improve the structure, usability, and appearance of their outdoor spaces with thoughtful landscape design and installation services. Serving Maryland and nearby communities, our team specializes in building features that bring long-term value to your property. Our team is driven by grit, ensuring we tackle every challenge with determination and a commitment to excellence.

Our work focuses on creating intentional outdoor environments through services like new garden bed installations, landscape redesigns, patios, walkways, and functional drainage solutions. Whether you're adding structure with a new planting bed, installing a patio for outdoor seating, or addressing drainage issues around your home, our team approaches every project with careful planning and attention to detail.

We believe great landscapes are built with purpose. Every project is designed to improve both the visual appeal and the functionality of your property, ensuring your outdoor space works better for how you live and use it. With a focus on ownership and wisdom, we're constantly learning to perfect our craft. If you're ever not completely satisfied, we promise to make it right.
