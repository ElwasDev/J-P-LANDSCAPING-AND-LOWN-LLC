Landscape Drainage Solutions in Kettering

Water management plays an important role in maintaining a healthy landscape and protecting your property. Poor drainage can lead to erosion, standing water, and damage to planting areas or structures.

We design and install landscape drainage solutions that move water safely across and away from problem areas.

Our landscape drainage services in Kettering include:

  • Dry creek beds that direct water flow while blending into the landscape
  • Swales and landscape drainage channels that guide runoff naturally
  • Downspout drainage systems that move roof runoff away from foundations
  • Infiltration and mulch basins designed to absorb stormwater

These solutions are designed to work with the natural grading of your property while supporting long-term landscape health.
