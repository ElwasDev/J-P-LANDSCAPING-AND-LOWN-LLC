Landscape Design Services in Central Maryland

Our landscape services in Central Maryland focus on creating outdoor spaces that are both functional and visually appealing. From custom garden bed installations and patio spaces to walkways and drainage solutions, our team designs and builds landscape features that improve how your property looks and performs. Every project is carefully planned to complement the natural layout of your yard while providing long-lasting results. The result is a landscape that feels cohesive, functional, and tailored to your property.

Mulching & Edge Definition

At J&P LANDSCAPING AND LOWN LLC, mulching and edge definition are part of a thoughtful approach to garden bed structure. We select premium mulch and shape clean, defined edges that frame your beds and pathways — reducing weeds, retaining moisture, and creating a polished, intentional look. We use Brown Dyed Double Shredded Mulch, a product that not only gives your yard a natural look but also holds its rich color much longer than traditional mulches. Whether refreshing existing beds or enhancing a larger landscape design, our team ensures every detail contributes to the beauty of your outdoor space.

New Garden Bed Installations

From concept to completion, we design and install custom garden beds that add dimension, color, and personality to your property. After assessing your site and vision, we prepare the soil, select plantings suited to your landscape, and create garden beds that feel natural and balanced. This service is ideal for homeowners looking to shape their outdoor style or introduce new living elements to the yard.

Garden Expansions & Redesigns

Reimagining your existing beds starts with understanding how you use your outdoor space. Our garden expansion and redesign service evaluates growth patterns, plant health, and spatial flow to create beds that feel purposeful and refreshed. We incorporate new plant material and updated layouts so your garden evolves with your lifestyle and environmental conditions.

Small Patios & Seating Areas

We design small patios and seating spaces to expand how you experience your property. Using materials chosen for context and durability — from natural stone to architectural pavers — we create gathering spaces that complement your landscape while encouraging outdoor living. Whether you want a cozy nook or a focal gathering area, our installations are both functional and beautiful.

Designed Walkways & Garden Circulation

Well-planned circulation changes how a garden feels. Our designed walkways and garden paths guide movement, define spaces, and enhance the visual narrative of your landscape. Choosing materials like natural stone or permeable pavers, we balance practicality with style to create paths that connect key areas with ease and elegance.

Garden Steps & Grade Transitions

Transforming slope and grade into a cohesive landscape feature requires both design insight and technical expertise. Our garden steps and grade transitions create safe, elegant connections between levels while preventing erosion and underlining the natural flow of your landscape. We pay attention to proportion, material choice, and long-term stability.

Permeable Patios & Walkways

Permeable patios and walkways are both an aesthetic and environmental solution. Designed to facilitate natural water infiltration, these surfaces reduce runoff while providing stable, beautiful circulation and gathering spaces. Our team selects systems that reflect your design goals while respecting the drainage demands of your site.

Decorative Drainage Features

Dry creek beds are designed features that manage water while enhancing your landscape's aesthetic quality. We craft natural stone channels and plant accents so water moves effectively, erosion is minimized, and the overall look feels organic to your yard. These installations serve both form and function.

Swales & Landscape Drainage Channels

Water flow should always feel intentional — not problematic. Our swales and landscape drainage channels are shaped and graded to gently direct excess water away from structures and vulnerable areas. This strategic design approach protects your property while promoting healthier soil and plant conditions.

Downspout Drainage Solutions

Proper roof runoff management begins below grade. J&P LANDSCAPING AND LOWN LLC installs thoughtfully designed downspout drainage pathways that move water away from foundations and prevent pooling. Our solutions balance effective function with landscape integration so drainage works without disrupting your outdoor aesthetic.

Infiltration & Mulch Basins

Infiltration and mulch basins are gentle, site-specific water management tools that capture and absorb stormwater where it falls. Combined with mulch and plantings chosen for your landscape, these basins support soil health, reduce pooling, and connect water movement to your outdoor design.
